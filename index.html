My approach

How I design learning

I follow a structured, evidence-based design process, always starting with the performance gap, not the solution. Every design decision is grounded in adult learning principles and aligned to real organizational outcomes.

01
Analyse

Training needs analysis, stakeholder consultation, and learner assessment to surface the real performance gap.

02
Design

Learning objectives, content sequencing, modality selection, and accessible, learner-centered design.

03
Develop

Storyboards, eLearning modules, facilitator guides, job aids, built with Storyline, Rise, Canva, and more.

04
Evaluate

Learner feedback, completion metrics, and Kirkpatrick-informed evaluation to drive continuous improvement.
